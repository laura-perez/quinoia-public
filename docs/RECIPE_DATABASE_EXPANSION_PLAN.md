## 📋 Quality Standards

### **Each Recipe Must Include:**
- ✅ Prep time ≤ 30 minutes
- ✅ Complete nutritional information
- ✅ Canonical ingredient mapping for shopping lists
- ✅ Equipment requirements clearly specified
- ✅ Serving size flexibility (1-4 servings base)
- ✅ Clear difficulty level (beginner/intermediate/advanced)

### **Recipe Validation Checklist:**
- [ ] Passes nutrition calculation accuracy
- [ ] All ingredients mapped to canonical taxonomy
- [ ] Instructions are clear and actionable  
- [ ] Cooking time is realistic and tested
- [ ] Dietary restrictions properly classified
- [ ] Equipment requirements are common/accessible
